Notaru

Put your phone down and listen. Notaru takes the notes.

Notaru records what is said around you, transcribes it as it goes, and turns it into structured notes: a summary, an outline, definitions of the terms used, and the key points. All of it exports to a Word file.

Get it onGoogle Play Coming soon toiPhone

A lecture, a team meeting, an interview, a conference, a consultation, a thesis defence: anything that is spoken and worth reading again.

What you get

  • A transcript that appears while you record, chunk by chunk
  • A summary of the whole thing, not just the beginning
  • An outline that follows what was actually said
  • Definitions of the technical terms used
  • The important points, gathered in one place
  • A clean .docx file, searchable and shareable

  • The recording does not stop

    This is why the app exists. You can lock the screen, put the phone in your pocket, open another app: the recording keeps going. A notification stays visible the whole time — that is what stops Android from cutting off the microphone.

  • Your data stays with you

    Notaru has no account, no server and no tracker. Your recordings, your notes and your key stay on your phone. Only the audio goes to the transcription service you configure, then the text so the notes can be written. No advertising, no analytics.

  • You need a key

    Notaru sells no subscription and takes no commission: transcription runs through your own account, with your own key. Groq provides one free, in thirty seconds, with no credit card. The app walks you through it, step by step.

  • Three languages

    Interface in French, English and Arabic, with full right-to-left support for Arabic.
